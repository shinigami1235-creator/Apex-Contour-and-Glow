Juvelook

Juvelook mixes PDLLA, a collagen builder, with hyaluronic acid. The hyaluronic acid hydrates the skin in the first weeks, and the PDLLA builds collagen slowly after that.

It suits fine lines, uneven texture, open pores and some acne scars. Made by VAIM in Korea.

What it does

Juvelook hydrates first, and builds collagen after.

  1. Act I

    The mix

    The powder is mixed with a solution just before your visit.

  2. Act II

    The hydration

    The hyaluronic acid holds water, so the skin looks plumper in the first weeks.

  3. Act III

    The collagen

    The PDLLA prompts your skin to make collagen, which shows at about 4 to 8 weeks.

Your fitting

Step by step at the clinic

  1. I

    The model

    Your doctor checks whether you have fine lines or a deeper problem and picks the Juvelook model to match.

  2. II

    Numbing cream

    The cream stays on for 30 to 45 minutes, and after that most people feel a slight sting.

  3. III

    The injection

    Your doctor places it in the planned areas, and you can do a few spots or the whole face.

  4. IV

    The first days

    Redness, swelling and small bumps settle in 1 to 3 days for most people.

  5. V

    The next session

    Most plans have 2 to 3 sessions, and your doctor sets the gap between them.

Questions

What people ask us

How long does the swelling last?

Redness, swelling and bumps settle in 1 to 3 days for most people, and a little longer for some.

When will I see a result?

The skin can look plumper early on from the hydration. Collagen changes show at about 4 to 8 weeks.

Does it hurt?

It stings a little. Numbing cream goes on for 30 to 45 minutes first.

I can feel a lump. What should I do?

It can come from the depth or spread of the injection. Don't massage it, and see your doctor if it stays.

Can I have it the same day as Botox or filler?

In some cases, and your doctor may space them depending on the area.

Do I have to do the whole face?

No. A few spots can be treated on their own.

How do I check it is genuine?

Check the Thai FDA number for that model, the label, lot and expiry on the box, and scan the maker's QR code.
