Radiesse

Radiesse is calcium hydroxylapatite in a soft gel. The gel gives some fullness on the day, and the calcium hydroxylapatite builds collagen over the next months.

It suits hollow temples and cheeks, a soft jawline and skin that has lost its firmness. Made by Merz Aesthetics.

What it does

Radiesse fills what has hollowed, and builds collagen under it.

  1. Act I

    The support

    The gel holds the area up, so a hollow looks fuller soon after.

  2. Act II

    The scaffold

    Tiny calcium hydroxylapatite spheres give your skin cells a frame to make new collagen.

  3. Act III

    The firming

    Skin grows firmer and thicker over 1 to 3 months, clearest at 3 to 6.

Not its dance

  • Fine shapingHyaluronic acid filler is better for volume and exact shape.See Filler
  • Volume with hyperdilutionWatered-down Radiesse firms skin and adds little volume.
  • Instant liftThe firming builds over months.

Where it goes

The seating plan for Radiesse

Face

  • Temples0.5 to 1 cc a side
  • Cheeks0.5 to 1 cc a side
  • Jawline0.5 to 1 cc a side
  • Chin0.5 to 1.5 cc
  • Smile lines0.5 to 1 cc a side
  • Whole face, for firmness1 to 1.5 cc a session

Body

  • Neck1 to 1.5 cc a session
  • Back of the hands1 to 1.5 cc a side

The amounts are typical. Undiluted Radiesse builds structure in the chin and jaw, diluted Radiesse firms and supports, and hyperdiluted Radiesse works on skin quality.

Your fitting

Step by step at the clinic

  1. I

    The plan

    Your doctor picks the areas and whether to use Radiesse full strength, diluted or hyperdiluted.

  2. II

    Numbing

    Numbing cream or cold first. Radiesse (+) also contains lidocaine.

  3. III

    The gel

    It goes in at each planned point. The visit takes 30 to 60 minutes.

  4. IV

    The first two days

    Use a cold pack for swelling, keep from pressing the area and take it easy.

  5. V

    Months 1 to 3

    The firmness builds as your skin makes new collagen.

Questions

What people ask us

Will I see it straight away?

The area looks a little fuller at once. The firmness shows at 1 to 3 months.

I had filler before. Can I have Radiesse?

In many cases yes. Your doctor needs to know what you had, where and when.

What age should I start?

There is no set age. It depends on hollowing, a soft jawline or loss of firmness.

Radiesse or Radiesse (+)?

Radiesse (+) has lidocaine added and is used for contour and the jaw. Plain Radiesse is used for firmness and the hands.
