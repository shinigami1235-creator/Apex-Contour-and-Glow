Vitaran

Vitaran is a Korean skin booster made of polynucleotides, the PN taken from trout DNA. It holds water in the skin and supports the cells that make collagen and elastin.

It suits dry or rough skin, acne marks, early fine lines and thin skin under the eyes. Made by BR Pharm.

What it does

Vitaran restores the skin, a little more with each session.

  1. Act I

    The formula

    Your doctor picks the Vitaran model and amount for your skin and the area.

  2. Act II

    The points

    The PN goes into the skin at planned points after numbing cream.

  3. Act III

    The repair

    It holds moisture like a gel and supports the cells that make collagen and elastin.

Not its dance

  • VolumeVitaran works on skin quality, and filler adds volume.See Filler

Your fitting

Step by step at the clinic

  1. I

    The plan

    Your doctor picks the model and amount, and checks for fish allergy, cold sores and inflamed skin.

  2. II

    Numbing cream

    It stays on for 30 to 45 minutes, and the injection feels like a light sting.

  3. III

    The injection

    The PN goes in at planned points. It takes 15 to 30 minutes.

  4. IV

    Day 1

    Small bumps, redness and a little swelling settle in 1 to 3 days.

  5. V

    The next session

    Most courses have 2 to 4 sessions, about 4 weeks apart.

Before and after

Etiquette for the evening

Before you come

Tell your doctorAny illness, medicine, cold sores, wounds, inflamed skin or fish allergy.
No alcoholSkip it for 24 hours before.
Pause strong skincareStop retinol and acids, and skip laser before your visit.
Keep your medicineNever stop a regular medicine on your own.

After you leave

  1. Early on
    • Keep from touching or massaging
  2. First 24 hours
    • No makeup
    • No heavy exercise
  3. 24 to 48 hours
    • No sun, sauna or heat
    • No alcohol
  4. After that
    • Moisturiser and sunscreen, and keep retinol and acids paused

Call us straight away if the pain gets worse, the skin turns pale or dusky, or you have heavy swelling, heat, redness or a fever.

Questions

What people ask us

Does it hurt?

A light sting. Numbing cream goes on for 30 to 45 minutes first.

How long do the bumps last?

Usually 1 to 3 days, and a little longer for some.

How long does it last?

About 6 to 12 months with continued treatment, and your doctor sets the repeats.

I have sensitive or acne-prone skin. Can I have it?

Often, yes. Heavy inflamed acne is checked and treated first.

Does it fix deep acne scars?

Vitaran alone may not be enough, since it restores skin more than it fills pits.

What goes well with it?

Botox for movement lines, filler for deep folds, Sculptra for firmness, Pico Laser for pigment, Ulthera for a deeper lift, Morpheus8 for pores and acne pits, and the Celeb Skin program.
