Before and after

Etiquette for the evening

Before you come

Tell your doctorAny medicine, allergy and recent laser, with how long ago.
Plan around eventsLeave a few days for redness or a bruise to settle.
Calm skinRest and skip irritating skincare for 1 to 2 days before.
Keep your medicineNever stop a regular medicine or blood thinner on your own.

After you leave

  1. Early on
    • Keep from touching or rubbing hard
  2. About 24 hours
    • No makeup
  3. 24 to 48 hours
    • No hard exercise or sauna
  4. Until your skin calms
    • No AHA, BHA or retinol

Come back to see your doctor if swelling or redness grows, the pain is unusual, or there is pus.
