Sculptra

Sculptra is poly-L-lactic acid, a powder your doctor mixes with sterile water and places under the skin. It shows little on the day and builds your own collagen over the following months.

It suits cheeks that have started to hollow and skin that is less firm than it was. Made by Galderma.

What it does

Sculptra works slowly, and the collagen it builds is your own.

  1. Act I

    The mix

    Each vial holds 150 mg of powder, mixed with 5 or 8 ml of sterile water before your visit.

  2. Act II

    The placement

    Your doctor spreads it evenly in the right layer and shows you how to massage the area.

  3. Act III

    The build

    The particles prompt your skin to make collagen, so firmness and volume return over months.

Your fitting

Step by step at the clinic

  1. I

    The plan

    Your doctor checks your face and works out how many vials and sessions you need.

  2. II

    Numbing

    Numbing cream or lidocaine first. Most people feel a stretch or a pinch.

  3. III

    The injection

    Sculptra is spread evenly through the area, and you learn the massage before you leave.

  4. IV

    The 5-5-5 massage

    Massage 5 minutes at a time, 5 times a day, for 5 days, with light fingertip circles.

  5. V

    The next session

    Come back 3 to 4 weeks later for your doctor to check and plan the next round.

Questions

What people ask us

Why do some clinics say a vial is 10 cc?

They count the water and anaesthetic mixed in. The vial itself holds 150 mg of powder.

Nothing has changed after 2 weeks. Is it working?

2 weeks is too early. Most people see a change at about 1 month.

Does it lift the face?

It firms the skin over time. An instant lift needs an energy device or surgery.

Does it help acne scars?

In some cases, together with other treatments matched to the scar.

How do I know it is real Sculptra?

Scan the code on the box with Galderma's eZTracker and check the Thai FDA number.

Is there food I should avoid?

No food is off limits. Skip alcohol and very hot or salty food for 24 to 48 hours.
