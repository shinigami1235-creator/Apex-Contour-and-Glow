Filler

Filler is a hyaluronic acid gel placed under the skin to fill a hollow, soften a deep fold or shape the lips, chin and jaw.

Your doctor matches the gel to the area, with a soft gel for lips, a medium gel for cheeks and temples and a firm gel for the chin and jawline.

What it does

Filler fills a hollow or a fold, and the face looks rested again.

  1. Act I

    The plan

    Your doctor looks at your whole face and starts with the area that changes its balance the most.

  2. Act II

    The gel

    Each area gets its own gel and amount, placed at points planned before the first one goes in.

  3. Act III

    The shape

    The gel holds the area up from underneath, so a hollow fills and a fold softens.

Not its dance

  • Lines from movementFrown lines and crow's feet come from muscle, so Botox suits them better.See Botox
  • Some delicate areasBetween the brows, parts of the nose and the upper eyelids are done only after a careful check.
  • Old lumpy fillerYour doctor checks what is left of earlier filler before adding more.

Where it goes

The seating plan for Filler

  • Under-eyes1 to 2 cc for both
  • Temples1 to 2 cc a side
  • Cheeks1 to 2 cc a side
  • Smile lines1 to 2 cc for both
  • Lips0.5 to 1 cc
  • Chin1 to 2 cc
  • Jawline1 to 2 cc a side
  • Forehead2 to 4 cc

The amounts are typical. Two areas together usually take 2 to 6 cc, and a large plan can be split over more than one visit.

Your fitting

Step by step at the clinic

  1. I

    Your face first

    Your doctor looks at the whole face and picks the areas, the gel and the amount for each.

  2. II

    Numbing

    Numbing cream or a cold compress goes on before the first point.

  3. III

    The gel

    The gel goes in at each planned point. The visit takes 30 to 60 minutes.

  4. IV

    Ice for swelling

    A gentle cold pack helps with swelling or a bruise. Keep from pressing the area.

  5. V

    Two weeks on

    The gel has settled and the shape is clear.

Etiquette for the evening

Before you come

Tell your doctorAny illness, medicine or allergy, and any filler you had before with where and when.
No alcoholSkip it for about a day before.
Bruising medicinesAsk your doctor about medicines and supplements that make you bruise.
Move the dateYour doctor waits if there is an inflamed spot, wound or infection on the area.

After you leave

  1. Right away
    • Gentle washing and skincare
    • Keep from pressing or massaging
  2. First night
    • Sleep with your head a little raised
  3. 24 to 48 hours
    • Exercise again
    • Sauna and steam again

Call us straight away for strong pain, skin that turns pale or dark, swelling that keeps growing or any change in your vision.

Questions

What people ask us

Does 1 cc make a difference?

In a small area, yes. A clear hollow or fold usually needs more.

Will more cc make me look overdone?

Some swelling at first is normal. The final look depends on the areas, the amount and the plan.

My old filler is still there. Can I add more?

Sometimes. Your doctor first checks where it sits, how much is left and whether anything looks wrong.

Will my face change a lot?

It depends on the areas and the amount. Some changes are small and some are easy to see.

Can I do several areas at once?

Yes, and your doctor may split a large plan over more than one visit.
