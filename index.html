The fitting room

A few pins before the gown

Every gown has a fitting, and every fitting has a few pins. Injectables work the same way, with a quick pinch at each point. Here are the five steps at the clinic, from the first talk to the follow-up.

  1. I

    Talk it through

    Your doctor looks at your face, hears what bothers you and picks the treatment and the number of sessions.

  2. II

    Numbing cream

    The cream goes on first and sits a while before your doctor starts.

  3. III

    The pinch

    A fine needle, a few seconds at each point. Your doctor tells you how many points before starting.

  4. IV

    Ice and rest

    A little swelling or a small bruise can show for a few days.

  5. V

    Your follow-up

    We check the result with you and tell you when to come back.
