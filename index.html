      Your visit

      1. I

        Consultation

        Your doctor looks at your face, asks what bothers you and picks the treatment and the amount.

      2. II

        Numbing cream

        The cream goes on first and sits for a while before your doctor starts.

      3. III

        Treatment

        A fine needle at each point, a few seconds each. Your doctor tells you how many points before starting.

      4. IV

        Ice and rest

        A little swelling or a small bruise can show for a few days.

      5. V

        Follow-up

        Your doctor checks the result with you and tells you when to come back.
