HArmonyCa

HArmonyCa puts hyaluronic acid and calcium hydroxylapatite in one syringe. The hyaluronic acid fills and lifts straight away, and the calcium hydroxylapatite builds collagen over the next months.

It suits the mid-face, cheeks and jawline when they have started to hollow or sag. Made by Allergan Aesthetics.

What it does

HArmonyCa lifts on the day, and the collagen keeps working for months.

  1. Act I

    The lift

    The hyaluronic acid fills the area where volume was lost, so it looks fuller the day you leave.

  2. Act II

    The scaffold

    The calcium hydroxylapatite gives the cells around it a frame to build new collagen on.

  3. Act III

    The firming

    Skin firmness keeps improving over the next 3 to 6 months.

Not its dance

  • Lips and under-eyesA regular filler gives finer control there.See Filler
  • Heavy saggingSkin that has dropped a lot needs a surgical lift.
  • DissolvingThe hyaluronic acid part can be dissolved, but the calcium hydroxylapatite part can't.

Questions

What people ask us

What side effects are normal?

Swelling, redness, soreness, bruising, tightness and small lumps you can feel, which usually settle.

How many syringes will I need?

Usually 1 to 2 for a first session, and more when several areas are treated.

Is it a collagen booster or a filler?

Both. It fills like a filler and builds collagen like a booster, so it is called a hybrid filler.

How is it different from Radiesse?

Both contain calcium hydroxylapatite. HArmonyCa adds hyaluronic acid for the lift on the day.

How is it different from Sculptra?

Sculptra builds collagen slowly and shows little on the day. HArmonyCa shows volume straight away.

How is it different from Profhilo?

Profhilo hydrates and firms the skin itself, while HArmonyCa adds volume and shape.
