Skinvive

Skinvive is a hyaluronic acid gel placed in the skin to hydrate it. It improves how the skin looks and feels and keeps the shape of the face as it is.

It suits dry or dull skin, early crow's feet, rough texture and dark tone under the eyes.

What it does

Skinvive holds water in the skin, and fine lines soften.

  1. Act I

    The plan

    Your doctor checks your skin and sets the amount for each area.

  2. Act II

    The gel

    The gel goes into the skin, usually with a local anaesthetic.

  3. Act III

    The glow

    The hyaluronic acid holds water, so the skin is more hydrated and elastic.

Not its dance

  • Volume and shapeSkinvive hydrates the skin while filler fills an area and can change its shape.See Filler

    Come back as booked so your doctor can check the result.

Questions

What people ask us

How is it different from filler?

Skinvive hydrates and renews the skin. Filler fills one area and can change its shape.

How much is used?

About 1 to 2 cc an area, and your doctor decides the amount.

Does it hurt?

A little. The needles are small and a local anaesthetic is often used.

Do I need time off?

No. Skip heat, alcohol and anything that irritates the skin for a while.

How long does it last?

About 6 to 9 months, depending on your skin and how you look after it.
