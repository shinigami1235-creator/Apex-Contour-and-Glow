Meso

Meso places hyaluronic acid, vitamins and other skin nutrients into the skin with a fine needle. The name refers to the technique, which has been in use since 1958, and the formula changes with your skin.

It suits dry, dull or rough skin, shallow lines from dehydration and skin that looks tired.

What it does

Meso feeds the skin directly, with a formula picked for it.

  1. Act I

    The formula

    Your doctor checks your skin and picks the formula for its main problem.

  2. Act II

    The technique

    Shallow points close together across the skin, or set points such as the 16-point technique.

  3. Act III

    The effect

    Each ingredient does its own job. Hyaluronic acid holds water, and vitamins brighten.

Not its dance

  • Melasma, freckles and deep scarsYour doctor assesses each one, since a general formula doesn't treat them directly.
  • A slimmer faceThe usual skin formulas don't make the face thinner.
  • An IV dripA drip gives fluids and nutrients through a vein, and meso works in the skin of the face.

Before and after

Etiquette for the evening

Before you come

Tell your doctorAny medicine, allergy and recent laser, with how long ago.
Plan around eventsLeave a few days for redness or a bruise to settle.
Calm skinRest and skip irritating skincare for 1 to 2 days before.
Keep your medicineNever stop a regular medicine or blood thinner on your own.

After you leave

  1. Early on
    • Keep from touching or rubbing hard
  2. About 24 hours
    • No makeup
  3. 24 to 48 hours
    • No hard exercise or sauna
  4. Until your skin calms
    • No AHA, BHA or retinol
    • Gentle moisturiser and sunscreen

Come back to see your doctor if swelling or redness grows, the pain is unusual, or there is pus.

Questions

What people ask us

Will I see a result after the first session?

Some people feel more hydrated straight away, and others need the full plan.

Are the swelling and bumps normal?

Yes, small bumps settle. See your doctor if the swelling grows or the pain is strong.

Will it make my face thinner?

The usual skin formulas don't. Check the ingredients with your doctor.

Will my skin get worse if I stop?

No. As the effect wears off, the skin can go back to being dry or dull.

Does it help acne marks or melasma?

Some formulas can even out tone a little, and your doctor checks which problem you have first.

Can I have it the same day as Pico Laser?

Sometimes, if the skin isn't irritated. Otherwise your doctor spaces them.
